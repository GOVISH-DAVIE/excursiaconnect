# Deal Room against the Vis a Vis decision paper

The decision paper *Connected Africa 3.0, The Deal Room, Agenda 1: Concept note and operating solution* (30 slides, for the Monday strategic working meeting) set against the Deal Room prototype on `main`, 11 November 2026.

The prototype already does the paper's core well:
- **Controlled circulation:** curated, consent-before-introduction matching, with documents released one at a time under consent and watermark, and an access log.
- **Two journeys and mandates:** separate seeker and provider applications, with mandates (ticket, sectors, instruments, exclusions).
- **Qualification:** review against a rubric, with a reason on every decision and a second decider.
- **The meeting itself:** scheduling, rooms and tables, check-in at the Deal Room desk, outcomes with owner and due date.
- **After the summit:** a pipeline to closure that keeps interest, promise, commitment and funds received apart.
- **Reporting:** a daily report.

The gaps are in the paper's vocabulary and scope. It is wider than capital-raising: it also covers procurement, government challenges, pilots and market access, and it adds formats beyond one-to-one meetings.
